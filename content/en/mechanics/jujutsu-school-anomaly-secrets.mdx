The gameplay loop of *Jujutsu School Anomaly* combines the psychological tension of observation horror games with the supernatural lore of modern anime sorcery. Players take on the role of a night watchman or student tasked with sweeping the high school campus across eight distinct shifts.

Your primary objective is deceptively simple: inspect each room, determine whether an irregularity has manifested, and take the correct protocol action.

```

A single error resets your shift progress counter back to zero, forcing you to re-evaluate the school environment under tighter time constraints.

To find the hidden courtyard NPC, you must equip the Celestial mobility artifact if available or execute a sprint-jump sequence across the outer air-conditioning units during Shift 4. The NPC offers bonus currency and cryptic lore hints regarding upcoming boss attacks.
