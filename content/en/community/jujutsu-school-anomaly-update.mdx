## Community Strategies and Pro Survival Tips

Mastering the mechanics introduced in the **jujutsu school anomaly update** requires deliberate habits. Veteran players and speedrunners rely on specific optimization strategies to ensure flawless runs:

- **Calibrate Your Field of View (FOV):** Set your in-game camera FOV between 85 and 95. A wider view allows you to spot ceiling warping and shifted wall sconces without turning your camera constantly.
- **Establish a Three-Point Inspection Routine:** Upon entering each hallway, execute a quick check:
  1. *Floor Level:* Check for misplaced shoes, blood splatters, or altered tiles.
  2. *Eye Level:* Inspect classroom door numbers, poster designs, and locker alignment.
  3. *Ceiling Level:* Observe fluorescent tube arrangements, vent covers, and hanging wires.
- **Manage Stamina Wisely:** Never drain your sprint bar entirely. Keep at least 30% stamina reserved in case an active entity anomaly spawns and gives chase.
- **Listen Before You Leap:** Stand still for two full seconds upon crossing into a new shift. Subtle auditory anomalies like backward ticking clocks or faint static triggers play immediately upon entry.

Applying these tips systematically will drastically reduce false passes and unnecessary shift resets.
