## Step-by-Step Patrol Route and Room Inspection Strategy

### 1. The North Corridor Wing Sweep

Begin every patrol by anchoring your viewpoint at the threshold of the North Corridor. Check the perimeter lighting first. If the lanterns display a purplish tint or emit no crackling sound, an anomaly is present before you even enter the main thoroughfare.

### 2. Classroom 2-A Verification

Enter the classroom and immediately glance at the teacher's podium. In a standard baseline state, a single attendance register sits closed on the center-right corner. Next, perform a clockwise sweep of the student desks:

*   Count the chairs in rows one through four (there must always be four per row).
*   Examine the windows for exterior silhouettes or blood spatters on the glass panes.
*   Verify that the ceiling fans are stationary; spinning fans in an empty room indicate cursed presence.

### 3. Central Courtyard Connecting Hall

This transition zone features high vaulted ceilings and hanging banners. Look directly upward. Entities in this shift frequently cling to structural beams or camouflage themselves within the drapery.

### 4. Storage Archive Inspection

The archive contains the highest density of small objects, making it the most dangerous room for false negatives. Check the sealing talismans pinned to the double iron doors. If any talisman is torn, folded, or burning without smoke, you have identified your target.
