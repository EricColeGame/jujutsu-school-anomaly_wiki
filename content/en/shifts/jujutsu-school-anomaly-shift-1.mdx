## Common Beginner Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

Even though Shift 1 is the starting chapter of the game, a high percentage of players fail on their maiden voyage due to avoidable oversights. The atmospheric tension often causes players to panic-report normal room features or sprint blindly through hazardous zones.

Review this troubleshooting matrix to avoid common pitfalls during your first night:

| Common Mistake | Root Cause | Consequence | Pro Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **False Positive Reporting** | Mistaking intentional baseline wear (e.g., washroom bulb flicker) for an anomaly | Sanity penalty, wasted report limits, shift failure | Treat the first 90 seconds as study time; memorize pre-existing imperfections |
| **Tunnel Vision** | Focusing only on eye-level items while ignoring ceilings and floors | Missing ceiling-mounted anomalies or displaced floor mats | Implement a three-point vertical scan (Floor, Center, Ceiling) in every room |
| **Excessive Sprinting** | Panicking when hearing phantom audio cues | Rapid stamina depletion, audio masking of real footsteps | Walk during routine patrols; conserve stamina exclusively for entity encounters |
| **Lingering in Distortion Zones** | Standing in a room while analyzing a subtle texture change | Gradual sanity drain and screen vignetting | Spot the anomaly from the doorway, confirm it mentally, and step back to report |

By eliminating these bad habits during your first shift, you build the precision and calm discipline required to tackle the far more chaotic shifts ahead.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do if an anomaly doesn't disappear in Jujutsu School Anomaly Shift 1?
If an anomaly persists after you have properly logged it, check whether the room requires a secondary interaction, such as placing a protective seal or resetting an electrical fuse. If it remains passive and your log was accepted, proceed with your patrol route; some environmental anomalies remain visible until the shift counter ticks over to Shift 2.

### Does Jujutsu School Anomaly Shift 1 have an instant-death entity?
While Shift 1 is primarily focused on environmental and psychological anomalies, community reports indicate that looking directly into distorted washroom mirrors for extended durations or cornering a shadow entity can trigger a lethal jump scare. Maintain safe distances and never linger near active visual glitches.

### How long does it typically take to complete Shift 1?
A standard run through Shift 1 generally takes between 5 to 8 minutes, depending on how quickly anomalies generate and how familiar you are with the baseline layout. Taking your time to memorize the school during this opening shift will save you substantial time on subsequent nights.

### Can Shift 1 be completed without logging any anomalies?
Yes, on rare occasions, RNG may produce a "clean night" where no paranormal deviations manifest across the campus. In this scenario, completing your required patrol rotations and logging the campus as all-clear will successfully advance you to Shift 2. Always double-check every zone before confirming a zero-anomaly report.
