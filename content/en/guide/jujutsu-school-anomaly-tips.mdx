## Essential Jujutsu School Anomaly Tips for Spotting Doppelgängers

When breaking down essential jujutsu school anomaly tips, focusing on facial and behavioral cues should always be your top priority. Anomalies rely on psychological trickery, often adopting familiar faces like Megumi, Nobara, or standard first-year students to lower your guard.

### 1. Scrutinize Facial Proportions and Animations
Community reports emphasize that mimics rarely get facial proportions completely correct. Look closely at eye spacing, pupil dilation, and mouth movements when they speak. An entity posing as a human may fail to blink, or their jaw might unhinge slightly during dialogue transitions. 

### 2. Dialogue Inconsistencies and Cursed Speech
Pay attention to what visitors say upon stepping up to the counter. Standard staff and students speak naturally about missions, classes, or curfews. Cursed anomalies frequently repeat scripted lines in an unsettling, flat cadence, or slip up by asking bizarre questions about the barrier's structural integrity.

### 3. Physical Glitches and Texture Discrepancies
Because this game utilizes custom 3D models and eerie lighting, anomalies often produce subtle graphic artifacts. Keep an eye out for extra fingers, inverted hand textures, or clothing collars floating away from the character's neck.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the most crucial jujutsu school anomaly tips for beginners?
The single most important tip for beginners is to prioritize accuracy over speed. Cross-reference every document using a consistent inspection order—starting with the photo, followed by name spelling, expiration dates, and stamp legitimacy—before taking any action. Never stamp a permit until you have verified all four elements.

### How do I identify a Smiley or Malevolent mimic?
Smileys and Malevolent mimics typically betray themselves through physical animation errors. Look for exaggerated, motionless grins, unblinking eyes, jerky head movements, or mismatched school uniforms. If their speech sounds pitch-shifted or layered with demonic static, seal the booth immediately.

### What should I do if the booth lights go out?
Power cuts and flickering lights signal high-grade anomaly presence. Rely on directional audio cues to detect approaching footsteps, keep your hand close to the shutter controls, and inspect any incoming visitors with extreme caution until power stabilizes.

### Are all visitors with unusual dialogue considered anomalies?
Not necessarily, but suspicious questions regarding barrier security or missing paperwork are strong indicators of an impostor. Always trust physical documentation over verbal claims; if their papers do not match official records, deny entry regardless of what they say.
