## Essential Survival Tips and Community Strategies

Veteran players frequently share practical tips on community forums regarding **jujutsu school anomaly how to play** without succumbing to panic. Implementing these habits into your runs will drastically cut down on avoidable deaths.

* **Calibrate Your Audio:** Directional sound is your greatest advantage. Over 40% of mid-game anomalies trigger subtle audio cues—such as floorboard creaks, air pressure shifts, or whispering—several seconds before visual indicators appear.
* **Practice Flashlight Discipline:** While the flashlight is vital for spotting dark classroom irregularities, leaving it on continuously makes you a beacon for aggressive entities in Shifts 6 and 7. Toggle it in short 2-second bursts.
* **Conserve Stamina at All Times:** Never sprint unless you are actively being pursued or navigating a collapsing corridor. Depleting your stamina leaves you defenseless if a mimic suddenly charges.
* **Check the Ceiling:** Many players fail sweeps because they only scan eye-level geometry. Cursed blood droplets, clinging crawler spirits, and misplaced ceiling panels account for some of the most overlooked anomalies in the game.
* **Maintain a Consistent Inspection Route:** Always sweep rooms in the exact same order (e.g., left wall, ceiling, center floor, right wall, exit sign). Standardizing your scan routine ensures your eyes notice misplaced props automatically.
