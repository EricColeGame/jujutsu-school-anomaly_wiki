### What should I do if an anomaly chases me in the hallway?
Do not try to sprint forward past the entity. Immediately turn 180 degrees and sprint back to the safe zone door you arrived from. Using Purifying Salt on the floor behind you will briefly slow the entity down if your stamina runs out.

### Can I unlock the True Ending on my first playthrough?

### Why do some anomalies not register when I turn back?
If the shift counter does not advance after turning back, you either misidentified a standard environmental asset as an anomaly (a false positive) or turned back before crossing the corridor's activation threshold. Always walk at least halfway down the hall before making your final call.

### How do I stop sanity from dropping so quickly in Shift 7?
Sanity depletes rapidly when lingering near active anomalies or staring directly at manifestation entities. To preserve mental stability, clear rooms efficiently, consume Sanity Pills from the infirmary when the screen borders darken, and avoid keeping your flashlight pointed at shadow figures.
