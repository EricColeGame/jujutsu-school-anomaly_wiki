## Core Mechanics and Anomaly Detection Rules

The foundational gameplay loop revolves around inspection cycles. You are tasked with monitoring the campus across successive night shifts, typically beginning at midnight and concluding at sunrise. 

During each round, you must thoroughly scan designated areas—including classrooms, practice dojos, courtyard pathways, and utility rooms—to determine whether an anomaly has manifested. 

According to community reports, anomalies manifest across four distinct tiers ranging from harmless visual oddities to aggressive physical threats. If an anomaly exists, you must flag it via your reporting tablet or terminal and retreat to a safe zone; if the sector remains untainted, you confirm normal status to advance time.

```
Patrol Sector Check ──> Spot Visual/Audio Anomaly?
    ├── YES ──> Log Anomaly Type / Retreat to Safe Zone ──> Shift Progression (+1 Hour)
    └── NO  ──> Confirm Normal Status / Continue Route  ──> Shift Progression (+1 Hour)
```

Failing to spot a distortion—or misreporting a completely normal room—penalizes your shift clock or triggers an instant ambush by lingering cursed entities.

## Room-by-Room Inspection Checklist

Success in this game relies on memorizing the default state of every campus zone. Even a minor change, such as a missing scroll on a wall or a training weapon placed at an unnatural angle, indicates cursed energy interference.

### 1. The Main Dojo

The dojo serves as one of the most volatile monitoring points in the school. Under standard conditions, you will observe two wooden training dummies positioned symmetrically on the woven tatami mats, flanked by weapon racks containing wooden bokken swords. 

Look for signs of movement: training dummies tilting toward the entrance, blood spatters near the ceiling beams, or missing weapons. Player experience confirms that if the dummies face each other instead of the entryway, an aggressive entity is actively staging an ambush.

### 2. Classrooms 1-A and 2-B

Academic wings are notorious for architectural distortions and object duplication anomalies. Always check:

*   The number of student desks arranged in rows (standard count is typically 12 per room).
*   Chalkboard messages: standard calligraphy lessons can suddenly warp into demonic incantations or red scratch marks.
*   Window reflections: verify that the exterior glass shows the outdoor courtyard rather than an infinite black void or an inverted reflection of your avatar.

### 3. The Central Courtyard and Torii Gate

Because the courtyard is large, spotting anomalies from surveillance cameras requires scanning specific focal points. Inspect the stone lanterns lining the central path; if their flames change from pale yellow to cursed blue or purple, an environmental shift has occurred. 

Additionally, watch the overhead torii gate. In later shifts, cursed silhouettes frequently perch atop the wooden crossbeams.

## Shifts 1 Through 8: Complete Walkthrough

Each shift increases both the frequency of distortions and the aggression of roaming spirits. Surviving the full gauntlet requires adapting your strategy as the school deteriorates into a complete supernatural barrier.

```
Shift 1-2 (Basic Awareness) ──> Shift 3-5 (Multi-Distortions) ──> Shift 6-7 (Stalker AI) ──> Shift 8 (Final Boss)
```

### Shifts 1 to 2: The Orientation Routine

The opening two shifts serve as a baseline calibration test. Anomalies here are largely static: a chair might hover slightly above the floor, a portrait in the hallway may change its gaze, or a door might refuse to open. 

Take this time to memorize the layout and establish an efficient patrol loop. Move through the main hall, clear the classrooms, sweep the dojo, and finish at the surveillance hub.

### Shifts 3 to 5: Escalating Distortion Waves

Beginning in Shift 3, anomalies can spawn simultaneously across multiple rooms. According to community reports and this **jujutsu school anomaly guide**, Shift 4 introduces auditory hallucinations designed to mislead players into checking empty rooms. 

Always rely on visual confirmation over sound alone. When entering hallway intersections, toggle your flashlight briefly; keeping it on continuously drains your battery and alerts lurking stalkers.

### Shifts 6 to 7: Aggressive Stalker Behavior

During Shifts 6 and 7, cursed spirits begin actively hunting the player rather than lingering in rooms as passive anomalies. You will encounter doppelgangers wearing familiar student uniforms whose faces distort upon approach. 

If an NPC approaches you with an irregular running animation or unnatural neck tilt, turn back immediately. Do not attempt to interact or complete paperwork in that sector until the corridor clears.

## Shift 8 Final Boss Strategy and Mechanics

Shift 8 departs from standard observation routines and transitions into an active survival encounter against the school’s core cursed anomaly. Once the clock strikes 05:00 AM on the final shift, the school enters an isolated domain space, sealing standard exits.

### Boss Phase Breakdown

1.  **Phase 1: Domain Confinement (100% – 70% Health/Time)**  
    The entity manifests in the central courtyard, summoning waves of shadow hands that emerge from the flagstones. Keep moving along the perimeter walls. Use talisman seals scattered on stone pedestals to disrupt the boss's aura.
2.  **Phase 2: Sensory Overload (70% – 30% Health/Time)**  
    The screen occasionally desaturates, and false visual clones sprint across your field of view. Identify the real entity by observing shadow reflections: only the authentic boss casts a dark aura on the floor beneath its feet.
3.  **Phase 3: The Desperation Purge (30% – 0% Health/Time)**  
    The entity aggressively collapses rooms, making areas of the campus inaccessible. Retract toward the main entrance shrine and interact with the terminal pillars in numerical sequence (1 through 4) to activate the barrier purge.

## Essential Survival Tips for Solo Players

*   **Calibrate Brightness Settings:** Do not play on default low gamma. Increasing your in-game or monitor brightness slightly makes shadow entities visible from the far end of the hallway before they aggro.
*   **Establish a Fixed Inspection Route:** Always patrol sectors in identical order (e.g., Courtyard → Dojo → Classroom 1-A → Classroom 2-B → Basement). Breaking your routine makes it much easier to overlook subtle furniture movements.
*   **Trust Your Initial Instinct:** If a room feels slightly off—even if you cannot instantly place what changed—check previous camera recordings or retreat. Indecision is the primary cause of sanity loss in later shifts.
*   **Manage Your Sprint Meter:** Never sprint across normal rooms. Save your stamina exclusively for emergency retreats when encountering aggressive apparitions or dodging attacks during Shift 8.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do I need a jujutsu school anomaly guide for higher shifts?
Higher shifts introduce simultaneous multi-room corruptions, auditory decoys, and aggressive stalker spirits that actively chase you. Relying on a structured **jujutsu school anomaly guide** helps you prioritize high-threat entities, manage stamina, and spot subtle environmental displacements before failure penalties trigger.

### What should I do if an anomaly doesn't disappear after reporting?
If an anomaly persists after logging it into your terminal, you either misidentified the category or reported the wrong room number. Double-check adjacent corridors, verify the sector name on your tablet, and re-submit the report before time expires.

### How do I identify doppelgangers versus normal NPC students?
Doppelgangers typically exhibit subtle flaws: inverted school badges, unblinking eyes, jerky walking animations, or missing shadows. If an NPC follows your character without speaking or matching dialogue prompts, back away immediately and flag an entity anomaly.

### Can all eight shifts be completed in a single solo session?
Yes, all shifts and endings can be beaten solo. Success depends on disciplined patrol routes, careful battery management, and strict anomaly verification rather than multiplayer assistance.
