### Anomaly Interaction Workflow

1. **Center Your Crosshair**: Position your central targeting dot directly over the suspect asset (such as an inverted wall portrait, a floating chair, or an extra locker).
2. **Engage the Inspection Prompt**: Tap your primary interaction key (**E** on PC, **X/Square** on console). A circular progress ring or verification prompt will appear.
3. **Select Anomaly Category**: Use the cursor or D-pad to designate whether the occurrence is an *Object Displacement*, *Audio Phenomenon*, *Shadow Entity*, or *Structural Distortion*.
4. **Confirm the Report**: Trigger the submission command to purify the sector or signal the monitoring ward. A successful report plays a sharp chime and stabilizes the sector's cursed meter.

### Stamina Economy Management

Your sprint gauge is finite. Depleting it entirely triggers an exhaustion state that slows your movement below walking speed for roughly 3.5 seconds. To maximize evasion efficiency:

- **Pulse Sprinting**: Tap sprint in two-second bursts rather than draining the bar continuously. This maintains high momentum while allowing stamina recovery between strides.
- **Audio Cues over Visuals**: Hostile entities emit distinctive localized sound effects (wet footsteps, wooden creaks, or whispering). Turn your character around only when you have clear running room ahead.
- **Staircase Navigation**: Avoid jumping while ascending stairs. In the Roblox engine, jumping on wedge stairs drains stamina unevenly and can snag your character model on railings.

## Control Troubleshooting and Common Fixes

Many community reports highlight common configuration hurdles when adjusting hardware inputs or mobile glass controls. Input lag, misregistered clicks, and camera drift can ruin an otherwise flawless anomaly report run.

| Symptom / Issue | Underlying Cause | Step-by-Step Resolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stuck in Inspection Screen** | Cursor focus lock engaged | Press **Esc** twice or hit **Left Alt** to reclaim cursor control |
| **Sprint Fails to Activate** | Stamina empty or Caps Lock conflict | Release directional keys, tap Shift once, and verify Caps Lock is disabled |
| **Camera Snapping Erratically** | High polling rate or Shift Lock conflict | Lower mouse polling rate to 500Hz; toggle Shift Lock off and back on |
| **Interaction Key Fails** | Interaction box occluded by geometry | Step 2 studs back from the door/object and center the crosshair directly on the handle |
| **Virtual Joystick Floating (Mobile)**| Dynamic thumbstick setting active | Set thumbstick to **Fixed** in the general Roblox mobile app settings |

Always ensure background recording software or keyboard macro managers are not intercepting system keys like Alt, Shift, or Function buttons during play.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I customize my jujutsu school anomaly controls on PC and console?
While the game utilizes standard Roblox engine mapping, you can rebind auxiliary functions through third-party controller mappers or adjust your input sensitivity and camera mechanics directly inside the in-game options tab. ### Why does my character stop sprinting randomly during chase sequences?
Your character stops sprinting when your stamina pool fully depletes. In this experience, running continuously consumes stamina rapidly; once empty, your avatar drops to a sluggish walk. Monitor your stamina gauge on the HUD and practice burst sprinting to keep reserves ready.

### How do I use Shift Lock in the game for better strafing?
Enable the **Shift Lock Switch** in the main Roblox settings menu (press **Esc** to open). Once toggled on, pressing the **Left Shift** key locks your crosshair to the center of your screen, rotating your entire character model with your mouse for pinpoint corner inspection.

### What is the quickest way to log an anomaly before an entity attacks?
The fastest technique is memorizing the quick-category hotkeys on your keyboard or D-pad rather than manually clicking through the report UI. Approach the object, press your interact button, instantly tap the corresponding category number, and hit confirm while immediately backing away toward the nearest exit corridor.
