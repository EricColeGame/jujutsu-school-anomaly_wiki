## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the most critical jujutsu school anomaly walkthrough controls for beginner players?
The most critical inputs are `Left Shift` for sprint management, `E` for rapid interaction, and `Tab` (or `Q`) for immediately toggling your anomaly log. Balancing your sprint stamina while quickly entering your reporting terminal prevents entities from cornering you while you log an incident.

### Can I rebind controls on PC or console?
Most core inputs follow standard platform configurations. While direct in-game remapping menus may be limited, PC players can utilize external software or secondary Roblox client settings to remap specific actions, such as switching sprint from `Left Shift` to a mouse side button for easier ergonomics.

### Why is my character moving slowly during Shift 5 and beyond?
Advanced shifts often feature environmental curses like gravitational heaviness or fear debuffs. If an anomaly is nearby, your character may suffer a temporary speed penalty. Counter this by breaking direct line of sight with the entity, toggling your flashlight off to avoid attention, and utilizing short sprint bursts rather than draining your entire stamina gauge at once.

### How do I report an anomaly if the terminal won't open?
Ensure you are not currently holding an active consumable item, as some tools lock off GUI activation. If the terminal remains unresponsive, verify your keybind status, take a step back from interactive door frames, and hit `Tab` once firmly. If a panic event is underway, you may need to reach a designated security checkpoint or safe room before the logging interface reactivates.
