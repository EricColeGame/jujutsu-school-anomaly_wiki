### Visual and Architectural Anomalies
These are static alterations to the environment. While they do not directly attack you, missing them resets your shift progression:
- **Inverted Wall Scrolls:** Calligraphy banners rotated upside down or bearing bleeding script.
- **Missing Architecture:** Hallways terminating in brick walls where doors previously existed.
- **Duplicate Lockers:** Locker rows extending beyond normal corridor dimensions.
- **Window Distortions:** Outdoor scenery replaced by static, voids, or giant peering eyes.

### Auditory Anomalies
Keep your game volume balanced, as sound provides early warnings before visual confirmation:
- **Distant Footfalls:** Heavy footsteps pacing rhythmically above ceiling tiles.
- **Reversed Bell Chimes:** The school bell ringing backward or at incorrect shift intervals.
- **Whispering Intercoms:** Radios emitting static accompanied by reversed character voices.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What triggers the jujutsu school anomaly true ending?
To unlock the **jujutsu school anomaly true ending**, players must survive through Shift 7 without failing reports, collect the secret Cursed Talisman hidden in the school, and successfully defeat the Prime Anomaly boss in the underground arena during Shift 8.

### Can you achieve the true ending in multiplayer co-op?
Yes, community reports confirm that you can tackle the game in multiplayer sessions. In co-op, team members can divide patrol routes across different wings. However, all players must survive the Shift 8 boss arena together; if the entire party wipes during Phase 3, the shift resets.

### What should I do if an anomaly spawns directly on top of me?
If a hostile entity manifests near your position, do not run forward. Immediately turn 180 degrees, break line of sight around the nearest corner or duck into an unlocked room, and wait for the entity's audio cues to dissipate before logging the report.

### Why did I get the normal ending even after completing Shift 8?
If you completed Shift 8 but only received the normal escape cutscene, you likely missed collecting the Cursed Talisman in Shift 7. Without this specific trigger item, the game bypasses the subterranean boss arena and defaults to the standard gate evacuation ending.
