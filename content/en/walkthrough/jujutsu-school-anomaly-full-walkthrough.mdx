Mastering the high-stress environment of Jujutsu School Anomaly requires methodical play habits. Keep these practical operational guidelines in mind during every run:

- **Create a Fixed Scan Order:** Enter every room and look in the same sequence every time: floor, left wall, blackboard/focal point, right wall, ceiling. Standardizing your scan path prevents blind spots.
- **Do Not Trust Lighting:** Many players mistakenly assume flickering lights are harmless cosmetic effects. In later shifts, light flickering patterns encode Morse-like signals indicating which adjacent hallway is unsafe.
