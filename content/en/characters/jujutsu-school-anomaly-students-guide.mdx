## Core Student Anomaly Types and How to Identify Them

Spotting abnormalities requires paying close attention to specific character model components. Normal students possess neutral idle breathing animations, standard dark blue or white jujutsu high uniform variants, and fixed walking paths. A critical pillar of our **jujutsu school anomaly students guide** focuses on facial structure and animation rigs.

Cursed entities frequently struggle to replicate human micro-movements, resulting in uncanny stillness or impossible joint articulation.

```
Visual Check: Face -> Uniform -> Shadow -> Walking Cadence -> Sound
```

### Visual Anomalies

Visual tells are the most common student deviations encountered during early shifts. Watch for inverted textures, textures that flicker when viewed at oblique angles, or uniforms missing official school emblems. 

Another telltale visual anomaly is shadow detachment: genuine student models project a dynamic ground shadow directly beneath them, while cursed student projections often feature delayed, missing, or disproportionately elongated shadows.

### Behavioral and Motion Anomalies

Motion-based student anomalies become prominent during mid-game shifts. A regular student walks along designated pathing nodes between classroom desks or down main hallways. 

An anomalous student might clip slightly through lockers, hover an inch above the floor tiles, or stop moving entirely the moment your flashlight beam strikes their face. If a student stares directly at your camera position rather than tracking your character's torso, treat them as hostile.

### Auditory Anomalies

Never play without directional audio enabled. Authentic students generate regular footstep audio matching the flooring material (wood planks in classrooms, ceramic tile in main hallways). Anomaly students frequently produce hollow barefoot sounds, rhythmic clicking, or reversed voice dialogue.

## Step-by-Step Reporting, Cleansing, and Evasion Tactics

Proper execution saves runs. Merely spotting an anomaly does not win the shift; you must record it accurately on your clipboard terminal and escape if the entity becomes hostile. Following our **jujutsu school anomaly students guide** protocol ensures you never waste precious seconds hesitating in narrow locker corridors.

### Encounter Protocol

1. **Maintain Distance:** When a student model enters rendering distance, stop moving at least ten paces away.
2. **Conduct the 5-Point Check:**
   - Uniform color and trim details.
   - Facial texture (eyes, mouth, symmetry).
   - Arm positioning and tool holding.
   - Ground shadow presence and shape.
   - Footstep audio alignment.
3. **Log the Finding:** Open your report menu immediately. Select the specific classroom or hallway sector, choose "Student Anomaly," and select the subcategory (Visual, Behavioral, or Auditory).
4. **Disengage or Cleanse:** If the anomaly remains stationary, turn back along your verified path. If the anomaly turns its head toward you, activate your talisman or retreat to the nearest checkpoint room.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do if a student model looks normal but makes no sound?
Missing footstep audio is an auditory anomaly. Standard students in the school always produce audible footsteps matching the floor surface. If a student walks in total silence, log it as an anomaly on your terminal and keep your distance.

### Why do I need a specialized jujutsu school anomaly students guide for later shifts?
Early shifts feature obvious errors like floating models or missing heads, but Shifts 6 through 8 rely on microscopic discrepancies such as delayed shadows, mirrored emblems, or subtle movement lag. A structured guide helps you perform methodical checks under strict time limits without panicking.

### Can anomalous students open closed classroom doors?
Most static and behavioral anomaly students cannot manipulate door hinges. However, during Shifts 7 and 8, aggressive Stalker-type student entities can force open unlatched wooden doors after a brief delay. Always latch doors behind you when retreating into classrooms.

### What happens if I make a false report on an innocent student?
Submitting an incorrect anomaly report counts as a strike against your shift log. Depending on your current shift difficulty, one or two strikes will immediately fail the run, causing the school's cursed energy gauge to overflow and resetting your shift progress.
