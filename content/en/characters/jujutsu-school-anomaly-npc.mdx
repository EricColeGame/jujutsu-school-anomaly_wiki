## Complete Catalog of Jujutsu School Anomaly NPC Variants

Anomalies within the school grounds generally fall into three distinct archetypes: Mimics, Distortions, and Spectral Manifestations. Identifying which category an entity belongs to dictates whether you should document and report it via your terminal, sprint to a safe zone, or break line of sight immediately.

### 1. The Sorcerer Mimics
The most deceptive threats appear identical to familiar characters at a distance. You might see a familiar figure standing near the courtyard torii gates or waiting at a classroom blackboard. However, inspect them closely:
- **Facial Irregularities:** Hollow eyes, stretched grins, or entirely removed facial textures.
- **Limb Disproportions:** Elongated arms reaching past the knees or joints bending backward.
- **Looping Idle Animations:** Jittery, hyper-fast breathing motions or rigid, T-pose transitions.

### 2. Corridor Distortions
Corridor distortions do not always take humanoid form right away. Players often report walking into a wing only to find a **jujutsu school anomaly npc** disguised as classroom desks stacked unnaturally, shadows cast in directions opposite to light fixtures, or inverted portraits lining the walls. If left unreported, these environmental distortions quickly coalesce into aggressive, chasing apparitions.

### 3. Cursed Entities and Phantoms
Unlike subtle mimics, phantom-tier anomalies announce their presence through atmospheric decay. The screen may desaturate, cursed energy particles will leak from door seams, and low, distorted audio frequencies will play.

## Step-by-Step Protocol: Reporting vs. Interacting with An Anomaly

To survive the later shifts, you need a systematic method for handling every discovery. Treating every entity identically will quickly lead to game-over screens. Follow this standardized four-step containment protocol:

```
[Spot Anomaly] ➔ [Assess Posture & Stare Meter] ➔ [Select Terminal / Countermeasure] ➔ [Clear Sector]
```

### Step 1: Establish Distance and Verify Baseline
When entering any room, stand at the doorway threshold. Check the furniture alignment, window reflections, and lighting. If a character model is present, do not sprint up to it. Verify whether the NPC has standard breathing animations and correct clothing badges.

### Step 2: Determine Entity State (Passive vs. Hostile)
If the **jujutsu school anomaly npc** is stationary and facing away, you have approximately 15 to 25 seconds to record its anomaly category on your clipboard or terminal. If the entity turns to lock eyes with you, your sanity or alert meter begins depleting rapidly. Break direct line of sight by stepping behind a pillar or sliding door.

### Step 3: File the Report or Deploy Cursed Talismans
Return to the nearest security station or use your portable log tool. Input the exact room number, anomaly classification (Visual, Auditory, Structural, or Entity), and severity level. Correct submissions instantly purge the anomaly from that sector and award safety points.

### Step 4: Relocate Immediately
Never stay in a room immediately after logging an anomaly. The cleansing pulse often forces neighboring entities into alert status, prompting nearby roamers to converge on the sector you just cleared.

## Pro-Tier Survival Strategies for Late-Game Shifts

When you advance into Shifts 6 through 8, the margins for error shrink drastically. Use these community-tested survival tactics to keep your run alive:

### 1. Master Audio Cues Over Visuals
Do not rely solely on your flashlight. High-level anomalies emit distinctive directional sounds:
- Whispering static indicates a nearby electrical distortion.
- Heavy cloth dragging across wood signals that a mimic is standing right behind an open sliding screen.
- A sudden drop in ambient sound (complete silence) means an anomaly has entered your immediate hallway.

### 2. Manage Flashlight Battery Discipline
Keeping your light source active at all times makes you an easy target for nocturnal entities. Toggle your flashlight in short, three-second bursts to scan room perimeters, then switch it off while moving through open corridors. Certain entities, particularly the *Smiling Sensei Mimic*, will instantly charge if a flashlight beam strikes their face directly.

### 3. Route Optimization and Reset Cycles
Never patrol randomly. Establish a clockwise loop: begin at the administrative offices, sweep the east classrooms, cross the courtyard, inspect the training dojo, and exit through the west dormitories. This circular sweep ensures you never get trapped in a dead-end corridor with a closing threat.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do if a Jujutsu School Anomaly NPC traps me in a dead-end room?
If an anomaly corners you in a room without a secondary exit, immediately break direct eye contact and look at the floor. Crouch in a corner away from the doorway and toggle off your flashlight. Most wandering entities will inspect the center of the room for three to five seconds before returning to their corridor patrol route, giving you a brief window to slip past.

### How do I know if an NPC is friendly or an anomaly mimic?
Friendly staff NPCs always occupy fixed locations, maintain continuous breathing idle animations, and speak through standard dialogue boxes with legible text. A **jujutsu school anomaly npc** mimic often has subtle anatomical glitches—such as reversed hands, floating feet, or distorted voice lines with reversed audio—and will never offer interactive quest dialog.

### Can you replay specific shifts to unlock alternate endings?
Yes, once you clear Shift 8 for the first time, the stage selector unlocks in the main lobby. This allows you to replay specific shifts to find missing lore items, speak with hidden NPCs, and practice identifying rare anomaly variants without having to reset your entire multi-hour run from Shift 1.

### Why did my report fail even though I spotted an anomaly?
Reports fail if you incorrectly categorize the anomaly type or submit the wrong room number. Some advanced entities relocate to adjacent rooms while your submission is transmitting. Always double-check room signage on the doorway lintel before finalizing your report at a terminal.
