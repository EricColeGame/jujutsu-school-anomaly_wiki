### Room-by-Room Inspection Sequence
1. **The Entrance Foyer**: Check the wall clock and the reception sign-in log. Anomalies often alter the hands of the clock or leave ominous red ink across the ledger.
2. **Main Academic Wing (Hallways A & B)**: Inspect locker rows. Ensure locker doors are securely shut and examine ceiling lights for discoloration.
3. **Classrooms 1-A to 2-B**: Verify that student desks are aligned in neat rows of five. Any inverted desk or hovering notebook indicates a cursed disturbance.
4. **The Training Dojo & Courtyard**: Watch out for distorted figures practicing martial arts in absolute pitch darkness.
5. **The Principal’s Office & Archive**: Check the antique scrolls and display weapons. A missing ceremonial sword often signals a roaming entity nearby.

## Player Tips and Troubleshooting Common Glitches

Because spatial-horror games rely on delicate lighting and precise physics triggers, players occasionally encounter odd quirks. Community reports frequently share practical workarounds to ensure your playthrough remains smooth and immersive:

- **Lighting Calibration**: Maximize in-game gamma if playing in daylight. Several passive **jujutsu school anomaly characters** hide in shadowy recesses beneath stairwells that are completely pitch-black on default settings.
- **Audio Spatialization**: Always wear stereo headphones. Footsteps made by Type-II roamers emit distinct directional pan cues, signaling whether an entity is above you on the mezzanine or flanking from behind.
- **Desync & Glitched Anomalies**: Occasionally, a door may swing open due to engine physics rather than an intended anomaly script. If no audio chime or secondary visual distortion accompanies a moving door, inspect the adjacent room carefully before logging an anomaly.
- **Stamina Management**: Never deplete your sprint meter down to zero during late shifts. Always pulse your sprint key in two-second bursts to preserve a quick escape reservoir.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are Jujutsu School Anomaly characters?
They are the NPC sorcerers, students, and cursed spirits found throughout the campus corridors in the Roblox horror game. Depending on the shift, these figures can spawn in their normal, peaceful states or manifest as distorted, hostile anomalies with altered appearances and behaviors.

### How do I report an anomaly correctly?
When you spot any irregularity—such as a misplaced item, a distorted student, floating furniture, or blood splatters—navigate back to your security hub terminal. Select the anomaly category and confirm your report to successfully progress to the next shift.

### Why do Jujutsu School Anomaly characters appear headless or inverted?
Headless models, inverted limbs, and hollow eye sockets are intentional anomaly variants designed to test the player’s observation skills. If an NPC displays any unnatural anatomy or reversed postures, they are guaranteed to be an anomaly and must be reported immediately.

### What is the most dangerous entity in the game?
The Type-IV Blood Hunter and the Shift 8 Boss Sovereign are the deadliest threats. Unlike passive illusions that stand motionless in classrooms, aggressive entities can pursue the player down hallways and require swift evasion techniques to survive.
